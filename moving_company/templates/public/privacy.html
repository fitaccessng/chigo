Your information and your move

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information Chigo Relocations uses to operate accounts, prepare move requests, and coordinate services.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, this may include account details such as your name, email, and phone number; pickup and destination details; move dates, property and inventory information; service selections; and messages, reviews, incident reports, and payment references or status. The site also uses session information needed to keep account and booking workflows operating.

How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, prepare estimates, process and coordinate booking requests, communicate about a move, record payment status, respond to incidents, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

Optional analytics

If you allow optional analytics, we use Google Analytics or a configured Google Tag Manager container to measure public page visits and a small set of move-planning milestones. Analytics is not loaded before your choice or on account, booking, checkout, payment, or admin pages. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, booking references, payment details, or private page URLs to Google. You can change your choice using Analytics preferences in the public-page footer.

When information is shared

Move details may be shared with assigned staff or service partners when needed to plan and deliver the requested service. Information may also be processed by infrastructure providers that operate the website, or disclosed when required by law. We do not ask you to enter card credentials in the current simulated payment flow.

Storage and security

We use reasonable safeguards for information handled by the service and retain it for as long as needed for the purposes described here, including account, booking, support, and legal requirements. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about or request correction of account information. We may need to retain some records where required for service delivery or applicable legal obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@chigo.com.
