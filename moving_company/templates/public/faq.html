Frequently asked questions

Answers about planning, estimates, and managing your move.

How do I get an estimate?
Start a booking, enter your locations and move details, choose any extra services, and review the estimate before submitting your request.
What affects the estimate?
The estimate uses the route distance, property size, floor access, and selected packing, cleaning, unpacking, storage, or special-handling services.
Can I save a booking before I finish?
Booking details are saved as you move through the steps, so you can return to the information already entered in the same browser session.
How do I follow my booking?
After submitting a booking, sign in to your customer dashboard to view its status, payment information, and move details.
How can I contact Chigo?
Email hello@chigo.com or visit our contact page.
